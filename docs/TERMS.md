# Mind_IA Terms

Mind_IA is provided as a software platform for automation, document handling, AI assistance and conversion workflows.

## Usage

- Use only data you are allowed to process
- Do not upload prohibited content
- Keep credentials out of prompts and logs

## Limits

- Premium runtimes depend on plan and policy
- Conversion engines may require external binaries or third-party services

## Availability

- This repository includes mock and local implementations for development
- Production deployment must provision real infrastructure and secrets separately
